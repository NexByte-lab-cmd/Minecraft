Welcome to the design room before launch. Think of this as the **Master Game Design Document (GDD)** for *Minecraft*.

Because the full game contains over 1,400 distinct blocks/items and hundreds of recipes, listing every individual color variant (e.g., all 16 colors of Wool, Concrete, Terracotta, Glass, Carpet, Shulker Boxes, Banners, Candles, and Beds—which alone account for ~250+ blocks) one by one would flood the document. Instead, below is the **complete, zero-omission structural blueprint** covering every system, mechanic, physics rule, block category, item class, crafting system, world generator, and tameable companion from the ground up.

---

## 1. World Architecture & Procedural Generation

The world is an infinite, destructible 3D grid of $1 \times 1 \times 1\text{ meter}$ cubes (**voxels**).

### Technical Grid & Storage

* **Chunks:** The world divides vertically and horizontally into $16 \times 16$ block columns spanning from $Y = -64$ (Bedrock floor) to $Y = 320$ (Build limit)—384 blocks tall ($98,304$ voxels per chunk).
* **Noise & Biomes:** Terrain uses multi-noise **Perlin/Simplex algorithms** evaluating six parameters: **Continentalness** (oceans vs. inland), **Erosion** (flat plains vs. jagged peaks), **Peaks & Valleys**, **Temperature**, **Humidity**, and **Weirdness**.
* **Three Dimensions:**
1. **The Overworld:** Standard earth-like biomes (Plains, Forests, Taigas, Deserts, Badlands, Jungles, Swamps, Mangroves, Savannas, Oceans, Snow/Ice Peaks, Mushroom Fields, Deep Dark, Lush Caves, Dripstone Caves).
2. **The Nether ($1:8$ travel ratio):** A cavern dimension sealed top and bottom by Bedrock, featuring Lava Oceans ($Y=31$) and 5 biomes (Nether Wastes, Crimson Forest, Warped Forest, Soul Sand Valley, Basalt Deltas).
3. **The End:** Floating End Stone islands void-suspended around a central Ender Dragon arena, expanding outward at $1,000+$ blocks to End Cities and Chorus fruit forests.



### Day/Night & Lighting Engine

* **Time Cycle:** 1 full day = **20 real-world minutes** ($24,000$ game ticks at $20\text{ ticks/sec}$).
* Sunrise: $0$ ticks | Noon: $6,000$ | Sunset: $12,000$ | Midnight: $18,000$.


* **Light Propagation (0 to 15):** Uses a flood-fill voxel lighting model (not PBR). Sunlight starts at level 15 and drops by 1 per block through semi-transparent blocks (water, leaves, ice). Artificial emitters (Torches = 14, Glowstone/Sea Lanterns/Lava = 15, Redstone Torch = 7) radiate outward, dropping 1 level per Manhattan block. Hostile mobs spawn at Light Level `0`.

## 2. Core Physics, Movement & Combat Rules

### Player Hitbox & Movement Physics

* **AABB Hitbox:** Standing = $0.6\text{ blocks wide} \times 1.8\text{ blocks tall}$; Sneaking = $1.5\text{ blocks tall}$; Swimming/Crawling/Elytra gliding = $0.6 \times 0.6\text{ blocks}$.
* **Kinematics:**
* Gravity acceleration: $-0.08\text{ blocks/tick}^2$ ($-32\text{ m/s}^2$) with $0.98$ vertical drag.
* Jump impulse: $+0.42\text{ blocks/tick}$ (reaches $1.252\text{ blocks}$ high—enough to clear a 1-block ledge, blocked by 1.5-block fences).
* Speeds: Walking = $4.317\text{ m/s}$ | Sprinting = $5.612\text{ m/s}$ | Sneaking = $1.295\text{ m/s}$ | Sprint-Jumping = $7.127\text{ m/s}$.


* **Fall Damage:** $\text{Damage} = \text{Fall Distance} - 3$ (in half-hearts). Mitigated by Water, Cobwebs, Slime Blocks (bounces), Hay Bales ($80\%$ reduction), Beds ($50\%$ + bounce), Powder Snow, Sweet Berry Bushes, and Feather Falling enchantments.

### Block & Fluid Physics

* **Gravity Blocks:** Sand, Red Sand, Gravel, Anvils, Dragon Eggs, Concrete Powder, and Pointed Dripstone convert into falling entities when unsupported below.
* **Cellular Automata Fluids:**
* **Water:** Spreads up to 7 blocks horizontally from a source block; extinguishes fire; creates infinite sources when two source blocks sit diagonally/orthogonally with a solid floor.
* **Lava:** Spreads 3 blocks in the Overworld (7 in the Nether); sets entities on fire; damages items.
* **Fluid Reactions:** Flowing Lava + Water = **Cobblestone**; Source Lava + Water = **Obsidian**; Lava + Soul Soil + Blue Ice = **Basalt**.


* **Explosions:** Cast rays from the epicenter checking each block's **Blast Resistance** (Dirt = $0.5$, Stone = $6$, Obsidian/Ancient Debris = $1,200$, Bedrock = $3,600,000$). TNT has power `4.0`; Charged Creepers have power `6.0`.

### Health, Hunger & Combat

* **Health:** 20 HP (10 hearts).
* **Hunger:** 20 Hunger points (10 drumsticks) + hidden **Saturation** buffer (up to 20). Regenerates 1 HP every 0.5 seconds when Hunger $\ge 18$ and Saturation $> 0$. Starvation triggers at Hunger $= 0$.
* **Attack Cooldown & Critical Hits:** Weapons have a swing cooldown bar; striking while falling (not on ladders/water/sprinting) deals a **$150\%$ Critical Hit**.

## 5. The Crafting, Smelting & Processing Systems

### Grid Crafting ($2\times2$ Inventory & $3\times3$ Crafting Table)

Recipes are either **Shaped** (exact spatial pattern) or **Shapeless** (ingredients anywhere in the grid):

```text
[Pickaxe]        [Sword]          [Furnace]        [Piston]
M  M  M          _  M  _          C  C  C          P  P  P
_  S  _          _  M  _          C  _  C          C  I  C
_  S  _          _  S  _          C  C  C          C  R  C
(M=Material, S=Stick, C=Cobblestone, P=Planks, I=Iron, R=Redstone)

```

* **Essential Foundational Recipes:**
* `1 Log` $\rightarrow$ `4 Planks` | `2 Planks (vertical)` $\rightarrow$ `4 Sticks` | `4 Planks` $\rightarrow$ `1 Crafting Table`.
* `8 Planks (ring)` $\rightarrow$ `1 Chest` | `8 Cobblestone (ring)` $\rightarrow$ `1 Furnace`.
* `1 Coal + 1 Stick` $\rightarrow$ `4 Torches`.
* `3 Planks + 3 Wool` $\rightarrow$ `1 Bed` | `3 Iron Ingots (V-shape)` $\rightarrow$ `1 Bucket`.
* `4 Gold Ingots + 4 Netherite Scrap` $\rightarrow$ `1 Netherite Ingot`.
* `7 Blaze Powder + 1 Ender Pearl` $\rightarrow$ `1 Eye of Ender`.
* `3 Paper + 1 Leather` $\rightarrow$ `1 Book` | `6 Planks + 3 Books` $\rightarrow$ `1 Bookshelf`.



### Secondary Crafting Stations

* **Furnace / Blast Furnace / Smoker:** Converts raw inputs + fuel (Coal smelts 8 items, Lava Bucket smelts 100, Blaze Rod smelts 12) into ingots, cooked food, glass (from sand), smooth stone, and charcoal. Blast Furnaces smelt ores $2\times$ faster; Smokers cook food $2\times$ faster.
* **Smithing Table:** Upgrades Diamond gear to Netherite using `Diamond Gear + Netherite Ingot + Netherite Upgrade Template`, or applies cosmetic Armor Trims.
* **Stonecutter:** 1-to-1 precision conversion of stone/copper blocks into stairs, slabs, walls, and chiseled variants without material waste.
* **Crafter (Automated):** Redstone-powered $3\times3$ crafting table with toggleable slots that ejects crafted items upon receiving a Redstone pulse.

## 7. Redstone Engineering & Enchantment Rules

* **Redstone Signal Logic:** Power ranges from `0` to `15`. Dust loses 1 signal strength per block; **Repeaters** restore signal to `15` and add $1\text{ to }4\text{ ticks}$ ($0.1\text{ to }0.4\text{ s}$) of delay; **Comparators** compare, subtract, or measure container fullness; **Redstone Torches** invert signals (`NOT` gate), enabling full Turing-complete binary logic (`AND`, `OR`, `XOR`, memory latches, piston doors, and automated farms).
* **Enchanting & Experience:** Killing mobs, mining non-smelted ores, smelting, and breeding drop **XP Orbs**. An Enchanting Table surrounded by **15 Bookshelves** (1 block away with air in between) unlocks Level 30 enchantments using **Lapis Lazuli** + XP levels, rolling modifiers like *Mending*, *Unbreaking*, *Efficiency*, *Fortune*, *Silk Touch*, *Sharpness*, *Protection*, *Infinity*, and *Frost Walker*.







### Generated Structures & Progression Loops

* **Overworld Structures:** Villages (with iron golems, bells, and job workstations), Pillager Outposts (with caged Allays/Iron Golems), Woodland Mansions (Evokers dropping Totems of Undying), Ocean Monuments (Elder Guardians inflecting Mining Fatigue III), Ancient Cities (Sculk sensors/shriekers guarding Warden territory), Trial Chambers (copper/tuff dungeons with Trial Spawners and Vaults), Strongholds (End Portal frames requiring 12 Eyes of Ender), Desert/Jungle Temples, Mineshafts, Ruined Portals, Shipwrecks, Trail Ruins (suspicious sand/gravel for archaeology).
* **Nether & End Structures:** Nether Fortresses (Blaze Spawners + Wither Skeletons), Bastion Remnants (Piglin Brutes + Netherite Upgrade Smithing Templates), End Cities & End Ships (Shulkers + Elytra).

### Status Effects (34 Buffs & Debuffs)

* **Positive:** Speed, Haste, Strength, Instant Health, Jump Boost, Regeneration, Resistance, Fire Resistance, Water Breathing, Invisibility, Night Vision, Health Boost, Absorption, Saturation, Glowing, Luck, Slow Falling, Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace, Hero of the Village.

## 2. Exact Engine Logic & Mathematical Physics

### A. Tick Loop & Chunk Simulation

* **Game Tick ($50\text{ ms}$ / $20\text{ Hz}$):** Every tick executes in strict order:
1. World time & weather update.
2. Scheduled block ticks (Redstone repeaters, water/lava flow updates).
3. **Random Block Ticks:** Inside loaded chunks (within 128 blocks of player), 3 random blocks per $16\times 16\times 16$ sub-chunk are ticked every game tick—governing crop growth, leaf decay, grass spreading, copper oxidation, and ice melting.
4. Entity AI, physics integration, and collision resolution.



### B. Fluid & Buoyancy Physics

* **Water Drag & Gravity:** Inside water, entity gravity drops to $-0.02\text{ blocks/tick}^2$ with $0.8$ horizontal and $0.8$ vertical drag. Holding Jump applies $+0.04\text{ blocks/tick}$ upward buoyancy.
* **Flow Vector Calculation:** Each flowing water block checks Manhattan distance (up to 4 blocks) to the nearest drop-off; entities inside are pushed along the resulting normalized gradient vector at $0.014\text{ blocks/tick}$.
* **Bubble Columns:** Soul Sand underwater generates upward water particles propelling entities upward at $+0.7\text{ blocks/tick}$ while refilling oxygen; Magma Blocks generate downward whirlpools pulling entities down at $-0.3\text{ blocks/tick}$ and dealing $1\text{ HP}$ damage per half-second unless sneaking.

### C. Mining & Damage Formulas

* **Block Breaking Time:**

$$\text{Ticks to Break} = \lceil \frac{\text{Hardness} \times 1.5}{\text{Tool Speed Multiplier}} \times 20 \rceil$$



*(If wrong tool or unharvestable tier, multiplier drops to $1.0$ and penalty factor changes from $1.5$ to $5.0$, yielding no drop).*
* Tool Multipliers: Hand = `1`, Wood = `2`, Stone = `4`, Iron = `6`, Diamond = `8`, Netherite = `9`, Gold = `12`.


* **Armor Damage Reduction:**

$$\text{Damage Taken} = \text{Raw Damage} \times \left(1 - \frac{\min(20, \max(\frac{\text{Defense}}{5}, \text{Defense} - \frac{4 \times \text{Raw Damage}}{\text{Toughness} + 8}))}{25}\right)$$

## 1. Block Science: Fluid Flow, Saplings, Bone Meal & Eggs

### A. Single Water Block vs. Single Lava Block Flow Physics

Both fluids operate as **cellular automata** with **8 fluid levels** (`Level 8` = Source block, `Level 7` down to `Level 1` = flowing slope, `Level 0` = empty).

| Property | Single Water Block | Single Lava Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizontal Spread** | Spreads **7 blocks** outward (`8` $\rightarrow$ `7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1`). | Spreads **3 blocks** in Overworld (`8` $\rightarrow$ `6, 4, 2`); **7 blocks** in the Nether. |
| **Flow Tick Rate** | Updates every **5 game ticks** ($0.25\text{ seconds}$). | Updates every **30 game ticks** ($1.5\text{ seconds}$) in Overworld; **10 ticks** ($0.5\text{ s}$) in Nether. |
| **Drop-Off Pathfinding** | Before spreading flat, water scans up to **4 blocks away** for a 1-block drop-off. If a hole exists within 4 blocks, water flows *exclusively* toward that drop-off instead of spreading outward. | Scans **4 blocks** in the Nether, **2 blocks** in the Overworld for the nearest drop-off. |
| **Vertical Fall Reset** | Any water falling straight down becomes a **Falling Water** block (`Level 8` equivalent) and resets its horizontal spread to a fresh **7 blocks** upon hitting a floor. | Falling lava resets its horizontal spread to a fresh **3 blocks** (Overworld) or **7 blocks** (Nether) upon hitting a floor. |
| **Infinite Source Rule** | If an empty/flowing block has **2 or more adjacent Water Source blocks** horizontally AND a solid block (or another source block) underneath, it converts into a permanent **Source Block**. | **Never forms infinite sources.** Removing a single lava source dries up the entire stream from the top down. |
| **Waterlogging** | Non-full blocks (Slabs, Stairs, Fences, Walls, Signs, Trapdoors, Leaves, Sea Pickles, Kelp, Chains) can hold a Water Source block *inside* their same $1\times 1\times 1$ voxel space without breaking. | **Cannot be lavalogged.** Lava breaks fragile blocks (torches, flowers, redstone, rails) on contact. |

### B. Sapling Growth, Tree Generation & Bone Meal Science

* **Sapling Block Mechanics:** Placed on Dirt, Grass, Podzol, Coarse Dirt, Mud, or Moss. Requires **Light Level $\ge 9$** and unobstructed vertical clearance above (`5–7` blocks for Oak/Birch, `14+` blocks for Spruce/Jungle, `4$\times$4` wide space for Dark Oak/Mega Spruce/Mega Jungle).
* **Two-Stage Growth:** Each sapling stores a hidden `stage` bit (`0` or `1`). Random block ticks first flip `stage: 0` $\rightarrow$ `stage: 1` (invisible change), and the second successful tick replaces the sapling with a procedurally generated **Log Trunk + Leaf Canopy**.
* **Bone Meal Fertilizer Rules:** Applying Bone Meal triggers a $45\%$ chance per use to advance a sapling's stage. On crops (Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroots), it advances `2–5` growth stages instantly. On Grass Blocks, it generates wildflowers, tall grass, and ferns in a $7\times 7$ radius; on Nylium, it generates Crimson/Warped roots and fungi; underwater on sand/stone, it generates Seagrass and Coral.
* **Leaf Decay Physics:** Every leaf block tracks its Manhattan distance (`1` to `7`) from the nearest Log block. If a tree's logs are chopped down, leaves at distance `7` lose their connection and randomly decay on block ticks, dropping **Saplings, Sticks, and Apples** (Oak/Dark Oak).

### C. Animal & Pet Eggs (Hatching & Incubation)

* **Chicken Egg:** Thrown projectile (`12.5%` chance to spawn 1 baby chick; `1/256` chance to spawn 4 chicks at once). **Warm/Cold Chicken variants** lay brown or blue-tinted eggs matching their biome.
* **Turtle Egg:** Placed on Sand/Red Sand (up to 4 eggs per block). Cracks through **3 visual stages** (`0` $\rightarrow$ `1` $\rightarrow$ `2` $\rightarrow$ Hatch) *only at night* (fastest between midnight and dawn). Zombies and Skeletons actively seek out and stomp on Turtle Eggs to crush them.
* **Sniffer Egg:** Placed on any block; hatches in **20 minutes**, or **10 minutes** if placed directly on a **Moss Block** (shows green crack lines before hatching a Snifflet).
* **Frogspawn:** Laid on water surface after breeding Frogs; hatches into **Tadpoles** after ~10 minutes.
* **Dragon Egg:** Gravity-affected trophy block that teleports $15\text{ blocks}$ horizontally and $7\text{ blocks}$ vertically in a burst of purple particles whenever punched or clicked.

Engine Architecture, Chunk Streaming, Greedy Meshing & Persistence1.1 Chunked World Organization & StreamingPartition the 3D world into chunks of size 16 × Y × 16 (for example 16 × 128 × 16 or 16 × 64 × 16), indexed by (chunkX, chunkZ) and local coordinates (lx, y, lz) where 0 <= lx < 16 and 0 <= lz < 16.Stream chunks around the player's active coordinates dynamically based on configured render distance, loading/generating new chunks and unloading distant chunks to maintain steady memory usage and frame times.Track a dirty flag per chunk (and adjacent neighbor chunks when edge blocks or fluids change) so only modified regions rebuild their meshes instead of regenerating the whole world.Apply camera frustum culling so chunks and entities outside the current view frustum are skipped during rendering.1.2 Face Culling & Greedy MeshingFace Culling: Do not emit faces between two touching solid opaque blocks. Only emit outward-facing quads adjacent to air, transparent/cutout blocks (where appropriate), or non-occluding faces.Greedy Meshing: Across each slice and face direction (+X, -X, +Y, -Y, +Z, -Z), scan matching block/material/light cells and merge adjacent coplanar cells into larger rectangles (width × height) to cut vertex and draw-call overhead while keeping accurate block hitboxes, selection outlines, and per-block interaction.Mesh Attributes: Generate clean vertex positions, face normals, texture/atlas UVs, block-type/material index, and corner ambient occlusion (AO) shading.1.3 Save & Load PersistenceSave modified blocks (delta map or serialized chunk snapshots), player position, pitch/yaw, selected hotbar slot, full inventory/containers, active game mode, time of day, GPS waypoints, custom blueprints, and world seed to persistent storage (localStorage / IndexedDB / save files).Support manual save, periodic auto-save, load saved world, and clean world reset.2. Visual Direction, PBR Materials, Lighting & Day/Night Cycle2.1 Physically Based Rendering (PBR) LookGive every block, item, character, pet, animal, villager, and monster crisp voxel silhouettes with layered PBR surface response:Albedo / Base Color: Distinct color palettes for grass blades, rich soil, stone/deepslate layers, wood grains, leaves, ores, metals, gems, and 16-color dyed blocks.Roughness: High roughness (matte) on grass, dirt, sand, gravel, wool, and raw stone; low roughness (glossy/smooth) on ice, glass, polished stone, gems, and metals.Metallic: High metallic response on iron, gold, copper, and netherite blocks, ingots, tools, and armor; non-metallic (0.0) on stone, wood, dirt, and organics.Normal / Bump Relief: Subtle surface height/normal detail for brick mortar, wood grain fibers, stone cracks, cobblestone edges, and embedded ore veins.Emissive Glow: Self-illumination on lava, torches, soul torches, lanterns, soul lanterns, glowstone, sea lanterns, shroomlights, redstone lamps (powered), end rods, campfires, and glowing ore/magma accents.2.2 Day/Night Lighting & AtmosphereDrive sun and moon elevation angles along a 24-hour cycle (0.0 to 24.0 hours) with presets for Dawn, Noon, Sunset, and Midnight.Update sky dome/background color, distance fog color and density, hemisphere sky/ground light, and directional sun/moon shadow casting smoothly as time advances.Couple darkness/nighttime with monster spawning rules and indoor/outdoor light visibility.3. Multi-Block Fluid Physics & Water + Lava Reactions5.1 Multi-Block Spreading SimulationRun a deterministic Fluid Tick Loop (tickRate ~0.10s–0.20s) over active fluid blocks and nearby chunks.Fluid State per Block: { type: 'water' | 'lava', isSource: boolean, level: number } where level = 0 for source blocks and 1..MAX_SPREAD for flowing blocks (MAX_SPREAD = 7 for water; 3..7 configurable for lava).Downward & Horizontal Spread Rules:Downward Flow (y - 1): If the block directly below is air or a replaceable/non-solid space, spawn/update a flowing fluid block below with level = 1 (fresh downward stream).Horizontal Spread (±x, ±z): When a fluid block cannot flow downward (or alongside downward flow), check horizontal neighbors. If a neighbor is air/passable and current.level < MAX_SPREAD, spread fluid with level = current.level + 1, updating affected chunks and rebuilding dirty chunk meshes cleanly.Source Support & Decay: Non-source flowing blocks recalculate their required spread level from adjacent higher-level/source neighbors; if their support is removed or blocked, excess flowing water/lava recedes/dries cleanly while source blocks remain until scooped or reacted.5.2 Water + Lava = Cobblestone & Obsidian ReactionsWater + Lava Source $\rightarrow$ Obsidian: When flowing or source water contacts a Lava Source (isSource === true), convert that lava source block into Obsidian, emit steam/smoke particles, and play a cooling/hiss sound effect.Water + Flowing Lava $\rightarrow$ Cobblestone: When flowing water meets a Flowing Lava block (isSource === false) horizontally or diagonally, convert the flowing lava block into Cobblestone (classic cobblestone generator behavior) with steam particles and sound.Flowing Lava + Water $\rightarrow$ Cobblestone / Stone: When flowing lava moves into or contacts water, convert the contacted fluid/lava cell into Cobblestone (or Stone when lava falls onto water from above) and mark affected chunks dirty so players can build reliable cobblestone generators and lava barriers.Bucket Controls: Using an empty bucket on a source water or lava block collects it into a filled bucket; using a filled bucket places a source block at the target position.6.
